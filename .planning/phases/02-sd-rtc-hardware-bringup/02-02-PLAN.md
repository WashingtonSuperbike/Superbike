## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-02-04 | I (Information Disclosure) | RTC datetime in filename | accept | Datetime is not sensitive information. Filenames visible on SD card are expected. |
| T-02-05 | T (Tampering) | RTC BCD data | accept | No validation per D-02. Garbage datetime produces a valid-but-wrong filename. No security impact — files can be manually renamed. |
| T-02-06 | D (Denial of Service) | Wire bus conflict | mitigate | rtc_init() does NOT call Wire.begin() — prevents bus reset that could disrupt CH422G/LCD. Only uses Wire.beginTransmission/write/endTransmission for register writes. |
</threat_model>
